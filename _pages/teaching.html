  1. ELEC70012 – Full Custom IC Design Lab
    Imperial College London

    This graduate-level course introduces students to the full-custom design flow of analog integrated circuits using Cadence tools in GPDK 45 nm CMOS technology. The lab is supported by a team of graduate teaching assistants, with my role involving coordination, technical oversight, and the development of core materials and lab content.

    Key learning modules include:

    • CMOS inverter and ring oscillator design
    • Voltage-controlled oscillators (VCROs) with enable functionality and post-layout verification
    • Differential amplifiers and two-stage op-amps with Miller compensation
    • Monte Carlo and PVT simulations, layout and LVS/DRC verification

    Students complete a structured design portfolio throughout the term, applying theoretical concepts to real-world design problems and submitting an electronic logbook of their work.

  2. ELEC70093 – Laboratory in ADIC (Advanced Design in Integrated Circuits)
    Imperial College London

    This postgraduate module builds on the foundational concepts introduced in ELEC70012, focusing on more advanced techniques in both analog and digital IC design. Students work with AMS 0.35 µm technology using Cadence tools to complete projects that integrate design, simulation, layout, and verification.

    The analog track covers:

    • Operational transconductance amplifiers (OTAs), mismatch and noise analysis
    • Switched-capacitor circuit design with PSS/PAC simulations
    • Parasitic-aware layout and post-layout verification

    The digital track includes:

    • RTL simulation and logic synthesis
    • Static timing analysis and place & route
    • GDSII export and design-for-fabrication practices

    I serve as the lead among a team of demonstrators, contributing to technical guidance, coordination, and delivery of the lab sessions.
