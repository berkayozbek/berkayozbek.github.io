I am a PhD researcher at the **Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab** in **Imperial College London**, specializing in **low-power analog/mixed-signal IC design** for biomedical applications.

My research focuses on developing robust and secure **wireless power and data transfer systems** for distributed neural implants. This includes:

- RF energy harvesting and self-regulating rectifiers  
- Adaptive impedance matching  
- Secure communication and autonomous ID generation  
- Integration with neural recording and stimulation systems

Prior to my PhD, I received my B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in **Electrical and Electronics Engineering** from **Middle East Technical University (METU), Turkey**, where I worked on low-noise, programmable analog front-ends for implantable hearing aids.

Beyond research, I’m passionate about teaching, mentoring, and promoting the next generation of biomedical IC design.
