2024

  • B. Özbek, J. Ge, P. Feng & T. G. Constandinou (2024). “An Adaptive Impedance Matching System with Absolute Peak Detection for RF Energy Harvesting.” IEEE BioCAS ’24, Xi’an, China, pp. 1–5. doi:10.1109/BioCAS61083.2024.10798328

    Abstract: Distributed neural implants with multiple wireless microsystems face varying orientations and distances to an RF transmitter, leading to inefficient fixed‑matching networks. This work presents a low‑power switched‑capacitor array and absolute‑peak detector to autonomously tune the impedance‑matching network, boosting rectifier output power by 11 % and 45 % under coupling ratios of 0.015 and 0.035, respectively, while consuming just 1.24 µW.

  • X. Chen, B. Özbek, P. Feng & T. G. Constandinou (2024). “A Reconfigurable High‑Efficiency All‑PMOS Charge Pump with Dual‑Phase Clock Scheme.” PRIME ’24, Larnaca, Cyprus, pp. 1–4. doi:10.1109/PRIME61930.2024.10559669

    Abstract: Presents a programmable all‑PMOS charge pump (180 nm CMOS) generating up to 10.73 V from 1.8 V, using an overlapping dual‑phase clock to minimize transition losses. Achieves 94.65 % peak PCE at 200 µA, with stage count and output voltage dynamically adjustable via clock‑gating.

  • J. Ge, B. Özbek, P. Feng, A. Mifsud & T. G. Constandinou (2024). “Flexible Electromagnetic‑Lens for Wireless, Implantable Brain Machine Interfaces.” IEEE BioCAS ’24, Xi’an, China, pp. 1–5. doi:10.1109/BioCAS61083.2024.10798285

    Abstract: Demonstrates two metamaterial‑based EM‑lenses (C‑SRR and square resonators) that enhance wireless power transfer (WPT) efficiency in implantable BMIs. C‑SRR yields a 12.07 dB PTE boost ex‑vivo; square resonators improve air‑gap PTE from 0.66 % to 16.58 %. Simulations identify optimal subcutaneous/epidermal placements and show > 88 % coverage under misalignment.

2023

  • B. Özbek & T. G. Constandinou (2023). “An Autonomous Zero‑Mask Unique ID Generation System for Next‑Generation Neural Interfaces.” NEWCAS ’23, Edinburgh, UK, pp. 1–5. doi:10.1109/NEWCAS57931.2023.10198170

    Abstract: Introduces a zero‑mask, on‑chip TRNG leveraging ring‑oscillator phase noise for high‑entropy IDs, verified by NIST‑800‑22. IDs are burned into antifuse OTP memory using an integrated charge pump/HV multiplexer, enabling fully autonomous, secure ID generation and storage for distributed neural implants.

2022

  • B. Özbek & H. Külah (2022). “A 9.03 µW Low Noise Highly Tunable Analog Front‑End for Fully Implantable Cochlear Prosthesis.” IEEE BioCAS ’22, Taipei, Taiwan, pp. 349–353. doi:10.1109/BioCAS54905.2022.9948682

    Abstract: A 12‑channel analog front‑end (85–6500 Hz) for cochlear implants consuming just 9.03 µW. Operates sub‑20 µVrms, with per‑channel tunable center frequency, bandwidth, and gain. Fabricated in TSMC 180 nm CMOS on 2.4 mm², demonstrating one of the lowest power analog cochlear interfaces to date.

2021

  • B. Özbek & H. Külah (2021). “A 1‑V Nanopower Highly Tunable Biquadratic Gm−C Bandpass Filter for Fully Implantable Cochlear Implants.” IEEE BioCAS ’21, Berlin, Germany, pp. 1–5. doi:10.1109/BioCAS49922.2021.9645031

    Abstract: Presents a sub‑13.2 nW Gm−C biquad filter (0.085 mm², 0.18 µm CMOS) with resonance tunable from 200–6000 Hz and Q from 1–3. OTAs operate in subthreshold, with bulk‑driven input transistors for low‑frequency coverage and folded‑cascode output stages for unity gain. Achieves 51.98 dB dynamic range at 950 Hz.
