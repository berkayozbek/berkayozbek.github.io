## About Me
I’m a PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/neural-interfaces) at Imperial College London under the supervision of [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.constandinou/). I have been developing low‑power analog and mixed‑signal integrated circuits for biomedical implants. My work focuses on architecting secure, ultra‑efficient wireless power and data transfer systems that enable minimally invasive, distributed neural interfaces, propelling innovations in implantable neural interface technologies.

## ⚙️ Technical Expertise

### Design Domains
- **RF Energy Harvesting & Power Management**  
  Adaptive multi‑stage rectifiers with absolute‑peak detection, integrated LDOs & bandgap refs, and ASK/LSK front‑ends—optimized for misaligned wireless power transfer.

- **Adaptive Impedance Matching**  
  Real‑time tuning of passive/active matching networks to maximize RF→DC conversion across varying implant orientations and heterogeneous tissue loads.

- **Secure & Robust Wireless Communication**  
  Lightweight authentication protocols, on‑chip TRNGs, antifuse OTP memory, and encrypted telemetry for end‑to‑end data integrity in distributed neural interfaces.

- **Ultra‑Low‑Power Analog Front‑Ends**  
  Sub‑10 µW LNAs, filters, and VGAs—leveraging single‑digit‑µW design techniques demonstrated in fully implantable cochlear prosthesis front‑ends.

---

### Core Competencies
- **Analog & Mixed‑Signal Circuit Design**  
  Low‑power building blocks: PMICs (LDOs, charge pumps, rectifiers, BGRs), oscillators/TRNGs, AFEs for biomedical implants, RF & solar energy harvesters.

- **Feedback & Noise Analysis**  
  Small‑signal modeling, noise budgeting, stability/feedback loop design, and parasitic‑aware layout (DFM) for robust analog performance.

- **Impedance & Power‑Performance Optimization**  
  Multi‑objective trade‑off analysis; MPPT strategies; dynamic load modulation; transistor‑level design for ultra‑low‑power operation.

- **Full IC Design Flow**  
  Concept ➔ schematic capture ➔ Spectre/HSPICE/LTspice simulation ➔ Cadence Layout XL ➔ Calibre DRC/LVS ➔ post‑layout verification ➔ tape‑out in 180 nm & 65 nm CMOS.

- **Verification & System Modeling**  
  Mixed‑signal testbench development; Verilog‑A behavioral modeling; MATLAB/Simulink for control‑loop/protocol simulations.

- **Lab Characterization & Test**  
  Silicon bring‑up; RF/analog measurements with oscilloscopes, spectrum & network analyzers; impedance matching & signal‑integrity profiling.
