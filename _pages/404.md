## About Me
I’m a PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/neural-interfaces) at Imperial College London under the supervision of [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.constandinou/). I have been developing low‑power analog and mixed‑signal integrated circuits for biomedical implants. My work focuses on architecting secure, ultra‑efficient wireless power and data transfer systems that enable minimally invasive, distributed neural interfaces, propelling innovations in implantable neural interface technologies.

## 🔬 Research

## ⚙️ Key Domains & Technical Strengths  

- **RF Energy Harvesting & Power Management**  
  Adaptive rectifiers with absolute‑peak detection, LDOs, bandgap references, and ASK/LSK front‑ends for efficient RF→DC conversion under alignment variations.

- **Adaptive Impedance Matching**  
  Real‑time matching network tuning to ensure maximum power transfer across dynamic implant positions and changing tissue impedance.

- **Secure & Robust Wireless Communication**  
  Lightweight authentication, TRNGs, antifuse OTP memory, and encrypted telemetry to ensure data integrity for implant‑to‑host communication.

- **Low‑Power Analog Front‑Ends**  
  Ultra‑low‑noise LNAs, filters, and VGAs operating in sub‑10 µW range, building on cochlear prosthesis front‑ends from MSc work.

---

- **Analog & Mixed‑Signal Design**  
  Low‑power analog circuits; PMICs (LDOs, charge pumps, rectifiers, bandgap refs); AFEs for biomedical implants; RF & solar energy harvesting; oscillator/TRNG design; high‑efficiency rectifiers; voltage regulators for wireless neural interfaces.

- **Core Techniques**  
  Small‑signal & noise analysis; feedback‑loop theory; impedance‑matching networks; transistor‑level low‑power design; parasitic‑aware layout & DFM; non‑linear and dynamic system simulation.

- **Full IC Design Flow**  
  Concept→tape‑out in 180 nm & 65 nm CMOS; schematic capture; Spectre/HSPICE/LTspice simulation; Cadence Layout XL; post‑layout verification; power‑performance trade‑off analysis.

- **Verification & Modeling**  
  Mixed‑signal testbench development; Cadence Virtuoso suite; Mentor Graphics Calibre (DRC/LVS); Verilog‑A modeling; MATLAB/Simulink for control & signal processing.

- **Tools & Languages**  
  Cadence Virtuoso • Mentor Calibre • MATLAB • Verilog‑A • HSPICE • LTspice • COMSOL • Altium Designer • Spectre APS/XPS MS • SystemVerilog.

- **Lab & Characterization**  
  Silicon bring‑up & evaluation using analog/RF lab instrumentation (oscilloscopes, spectrum/network analyzers); RF power & impedance matching; signal integrity and frequency‑response profiling for wireless implants.
