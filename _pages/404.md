## 🧑‍💻 About Berkay Özbek

I’m **Berkay Özbek**, a PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/neural-interfaces) under the supervision of [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.constandinou/) at Imperial College London, where I have been developing low-power analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits for biomedical implants. My work focuses on architecting secure, ultra-efficient wireless power and data transfer systems that enable minimally invasive, distributed neural interfaces, propelling innovations in implantable neural interface technologies.

### <span class="section-icon">⚙️</span> Professional Summary

Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Designer with 7+ years’ experience and multiple 180 nm & 65 nm tape-outs. Expertise includes:

- **Ultra-Low-Power Analog:** LDOs, BGRs, Charge Pumps, DACs, Op-amps/OTAs, PGAs, LNAs, Gm-C Filters, Current References/Mirrors, ESD Protection
- **RF & Mixed-Signal:** VCOs, PLLs, CDRs, RF rectifiers, ASK/LSK Front-ends, Encoders/Decoders, TRNGs & OTP memory
- **Tools & Flow:** Cadence Virtuoso (schematic → layout → tape-out), Verilog-A Modeling, Spectre/HSPICE/LTspice Simulation, Calibre DRC/LVS verification
