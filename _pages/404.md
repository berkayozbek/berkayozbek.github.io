## About Me

I’m a PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/neural-interfaces) at Imperial College London under the supervision of [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.constandinou/). I design ultra‑low‑power analog/mixed‑signal ICs for minimally invasive neural implants. My goal is to unlock truly wireless, secure, distributed brain‑machine interfaces.

## 🔬 Research

### Key Domains
- **RF Energy Harvesting & Power Management**  
  Adaptive rectifiers with absolute‑peak detection, LDOs, bandgap refs, and ASK/LSK front‑ends for optimal RF→DC conversion under misalignment.
- **Adaptive Impedance Matching**  
  Real‑time tuning of matching networks to maintain maximal power transfer across varying implant orientations and tissue impedances.
- **Secure & Robust Wireless Communication**  
  Lightweight authentication protocols, TRNGs, antifuse OTP, and encrypted telemetry for implant‑to‑host data integrity.
- **Low‑Power Analog Front‑Ends**  
  Ultra‑low‑noise LNAs, filters, and VGAs in single‑digit‑µW regimes—built on my MSc work for fully implantable cochlear prostheses.
