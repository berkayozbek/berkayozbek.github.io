## About Me

I’m **Berkay Özbek**, PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces Lab](/research/) at Imperial College London under [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](/supervisor/). I design ultra‑low‑power analog/mixed‑signal ICs—energy‑harvesting PMUs, adaptive rectifiers, impedance‑matching networks, secure wireless links, and low‑noise front‑ends—to enable fully wireless neural implants.

## 🔹 Professional Summary
7+ years’ experience in full‑flow Cadence Virtuoso design/layout, multiple 180 nm & 65 nm tape‑outs, PMICs (LDOs, charge pumps, BGRs), RF energy harvesting, TRNGs & OTP security, and post‑layout verification.
