I’m **Berkay Özbek**, a PhD candidate in the [Next Generation Neural Interfaces (NGNI) Lab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/neural-interfaces) at Imperial College London under the supervision of [Prof. Timothy G. Constandinou](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.constandinou/). I design ultra‑low‑power analog/mixed‑signal ICs—spanning energy‑harvesting PMUs, adaptive rectifiers, impedance‑matching networks, secure wireless links, and low‑noise front‑ends—to enable minimally invasive, fully wireless neural implants.

## 🔹 Professional Summary
Highly motivated Analog/Mixed‑Signal IC Designer with 7+ years’ experience and multiple tape‑outs in 180 nm & 65 nm CMOS. Expert in low‑power building blocks (LDOs, charge pumps, bandgaps), RF energy harvesting, TRNGs & OTP, full‑flow Cadence Virtuoso design/layout, Verilog‑A modeling, and post‑layout verification.

## 🔬 Research Focus
- **RF Energy Harvesting & PMU Design**  
  Passive rectifiers (92 % PCE) + MPPT via impedance tuning, LDOs, bandgap refs, clock/data recovery.
- **Adaptive Impedance Matching**  
  Real‑time network tuning for maximal RF→DC conversion across misalignments and tissue loads.
- **Secure Wireless Telemetry**  
  Lightweight auth, true‑random ID (TRNG), antifuse OTP, charge‑pump‑driven non‑volatile storage.
- **Ultra‑Low‑Noise Analog Front‑Ends**  
  LNAs, VGAs & filters in single‑digit µW regimes, with demonstrations in implantable cochlear interfaces.
